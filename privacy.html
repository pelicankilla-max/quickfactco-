Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

QuickFactCo ("we", "us") operates a short-form video content channel and internal automated publishing tooling for our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.

Data we collect

Our publishing tooling does not collect personal data from viewers or third parties. It uses OAuth credentials we control to publish content to our own accounts on YouTube (via YouTube Data API), TikTok (via Content Posting API), and Instagram (via Meta Graph API). We may receive public post performance metrics (views, likes, comments, retention) from these platforms' own analytics APIs for our own content.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to viewers, followers, or any third party. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics on this site.

Third-party platforms

Content published through our tooling is subject to the privacy policies of YouTube/Google, TikTok, and Meta/Instagram respectively.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact info on our channel profile.
